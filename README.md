StoryCraftAI is a feature-rich web platform designed to empower users in story writing and creative collaboration by leveraging advanced AI tools and vibrant community engagement features.

***

### Project Overview

StoryCraftAI is your all-in-one creative writing assistant, combining AI-powered story generation, feedback loops, live collaboration features, and intuitive management tools. Writers of all backgrounds can start, refine, and share their work effortlessly while engaging with a supportive community.

***

### Key Features

- **AI Writing Modes**
  - **Generate**: Instantly create scenes or characters by describing prompts. Use AI-generated suggestions and customize them before adding to your story.
  - **Chat**: Brainstorm story ideas, discuss writing hurdles, and converse about your creative journey with the TaleWeaver AI chatbot.
  - **Feedback**: Receive targeted advice on pacing, character development, and dialogue. Upload your draft to unlock actionable AI-driven feedback.

- **Community & Engagement**
  - **Publish & Share**: Release your stories to the community, gain new readers, and be discovered.
  - **Draft Mode**: Work privately on your drafts and publish only when you’re ready.
  - **Likes and Comments**: React to community stories, comment, and initiate discussions with other writers.

- **Story Management**
  - **Flexible Editing**: Modify your work anytime, even after publishing.
  - **Auto-save**: Never lose progress—your writing is backed up in real-time.
  - **Preview and Recovery**: Preview your work before release and restore older versions anytime.

- **Guided Creative Journey**
  - Step-by-step workflow from starting a blank canvas to publishing or keeping your work private.
  - Seamless use of AI tools and feedback at every stage of your writing journey.

- **User Experience**
  - Modern, responsive interface optimized for both desktop and mobile.
  - Fast navigation between story creation, community, and management screens.

***

### How To Use

1. **Start Writing**
   - Visit [https://storycraftai.nivaash.com/](https://storycraftai.nivaash.com/) and click “Start Writing Now” to begin your story.
2. **Use AI Tools**
   - Select your preferred mode—Generate, Chat, or Feedback—depending on your creative needs.
3. **Refine & Edit**
   - Polish your story using StoryCraftAI’s editing and feedback features.
4. **Publish or Keep Private**
   - Choose to share with the community or keep your drafts private until ready.
5. **Engage with Community**
   - Explore published works, leave likes and comments, and connect with other writers.

### Tech Stack

- **Lucide Icons**: Modern icon set for all feature visuals.
- **Custom Backend**: AI model integration for story writing features.
- **Responsive Design**: Optimized for all device sizes.
